title: "Distraction Blocking"
description: "Block distracting websites and apps — during focus, with a daily limit, or on a schedule"
---

The Focuh desktop app for macOS blocks websites and apps you find distracting. Every entry in your block list has three independent rules you can combine:

- **During focus** — blocked while a focus session is running. This is the default for every entry.
- **Daily limit** — a number of minutes per day, counted while the site or app is in front of you. Once it's used up, the entry is blocked until midnight. There is no override.
- **Scheduled** — blocked during your blocking hours, focus session or not.

## How It Works

1. Download the [Focuh desktop app](/download)
2. Open Preferences → Blocking and add a site or app to your block list
3. Select an entry to add a daily limit or put it on the schedule
4. A blocked site shows a calm page telling you why, and a blocked app is closed

## Daily limits

Set a limit in minutes — any number up to a full day. Time only counts while the site or app is the frontmost window, and it pauses when you step away from the Mac — except while audio is playing, so a video you're watching still counts.

You get a notification with 5 minutes left and another with 1 minute left. When the limit is reached, the tab is redirected and any app is closed. Limits reset at midnight, local time.

Usage is counted on this Mac. If you use Focuh on two Macs, each keeps its own count.

## Blocking hours

There is one schedule for everything: pick the days and a start and end time under Preferences → Blocking → Schedule (Mon–Fri, 09:00–17:00 by default). Entries opt in with their own **Scheduled** switch.

## Keeping Focuh running

Limits and the schedule only work while Focuh is open. Closing the window doesn't quit Focuh: it keeps running in the menu bar, without a Dock icon, and clicking the menu bar icon brings the window back. Under Preferences → General → Startup you can turn on **Open at login** so Focuh starts in the background when you log in.

Quitting Focuh shows which limits would stop being enforced before it exits.

## Managing Your Block List

Your block list syncs with your account, so it's the same on every Mac you sign in to. Today's usage counters stay on each Mac.

## Common Sites to Block

Popular productivity killers:

- `twitter.com` / `x.com`
- `reddit.com`
- `youtube.com`
- `facebook.com`
- `instagram.com`
- `tiktok.com`
- `news.ycombinator.com`

<Note>
Entering `x.com` also covers `twitter.com` and `t.co`; `youtube.com` covers `youtu.be` and `m.youtube.com`. Subdomains of an entry are matched too.
</Note>

## Desktop App Features

- **macOS (native)**: System-wide blocking across Safari, Chrome, Arc, Brave and other browsers, plus app blocking
  - Uses **Accessibility + Automation** (AppleScript) — no admin password, just one-time permissions
- **Browser extension** (Chrome/Edge): enforces the *during focus* rule only; daily limits and the schedule are desktop-app features
